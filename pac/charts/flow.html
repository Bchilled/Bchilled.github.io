1 · The wheel — 7 stats

In wheel order (placement follows the MTG colour wheel — neighbours and opposites are set by strengths, weaknesses and builds):

Strength — Vitality — Spirit — Intellect — Agility — Speciality — Nature — and back to Strength.

One chart for everything: heroes are filed under the stat that defines them, and each stat carries its own + slot and its own aligned stone (sections 3 and 4).

2 · Shades — 3 per stat, 21 in all

Each stat splits into 3 colour shades, and the stat's sub-stats are split between the shades. Heroes are placed under their strongest sub-stat — a hero's home is the shade of the sub-stat they lean on hardest.

5 · Spin offers — how the chart deals

Level 1 — the spin

The chart spins and lands somewhere — you pick from that 1.

Level 2 — one away

The second spin lands 1 away from the first — on one side or the other, 50/50 which side.

Level 3 — opposite

Level 3 is opposite — giving 3 choices opposite the first picks.

Because the spins walk neighbours and then jump opposite, each stat is placed strategically on the wheel — separated by opposing strengths and builds.

7 · Unlock flow — access is earned, the unlock rolls

  1. 1Explore their zone. You don't get access to unlock a hero unless you explore their zone first.
  2. 2Defeat them — and the bosses. The hero and the realm's bosses go down.
  3. 3Chance to spin. Then you get a chance to spin to unlock the hero from that realm.
  4. 4The spin decides. A spin can give you a variant of what you already have — explore and fight for access, and it's still always random.

Buying instead? A hero in a realm far away costs more rings — +5 per distance (see Economies).
