# PAC — the 12 trees (v0.1, 2026-10-19)

Every power, upgrade, form and system from the brainstorm lives in ONE of 12 trees.
Level-up choices draw from trees; deep investment unlocks capstones.

1. **Maw** — the mouth is the weapon. Chomp radius, bite damage, mouth-size
   upgrades, vampire teeth (lifesteal), scaling: +power per X eaten.
2. **Velocity** — speed upgrades, momentum, combo preservation, chomp-dash,
   ghost-phase slips. Fast is alive.
3. **Body** — health upgrades, regen, shields, SIZE shifting (small = fast/narrow,
   large = slow/huge maw), armor.
4. **Elements** — fire cone, frost aura/slows, laser beam; elemental chomps and trails.
5. **Arsenal** — weapons: Thor's hammer (chain-lightning throw), bow & arrow,
   chameleon tongue (long-range grab/eat), weapon cooldowns and ricochets.
6. **Theft** — Kirby line: steal abilities from the eaten, hold more stolen powers,
   BLUE saved slots (store a power, consume on demand), +1 slot upgrades.
7. **Forms** — transformations: Mrs. Pac, Hulk Pac, Wolverine Pac, Werewolf,
   Rubber-Ducky Pac, Kirby Pac. Longer forms, faster form-switching; capstone: form fusion.
8. **Death** — necromancer line: the eaten rise as allies, undead capacity, ghost
   servants, corpse explosions.
9. **Beasts** — capture line: throw balls, pet slots (+1 Ball Master), pet damage/
   loyalty, the Ash hat capstone (pets evolve).
10. **Mounts** — the horse: ride speed, trample damage, mounted chomping, mount
    stamina; capstone: ghost-horse (ride through the swarm).
11. **Ghost Hunter** — anti-swarm: eat-mode duration, pellet frequency, ghost damage,
    fear radius, swarm thinning (chain-eat bonuses). The tree that fights the clock.
12. **Greed** — the economy: magnet, dot value, XP gain, fruit/elite drop rates,
    combo banking. Rich runs end loud.

Design rule: no tree is pure stats — each one changes HOW you play by its mid-tier.

## The Ring (owner rule, 2026-10-19)

The 12 trees form a RING. Taking your first node in a tree **opens the two trees
beside it** — level-up offers only come from open trees, so builds spread around
the ring instead of teleporting across it. Deep in one tree + mid in a neighbour
= hybrid builds (that's where the fusion capstones live).

Ring order (13): Maw — Velocity — **Kart** — Ghost Hunter — Greed — Beasts —
Mounts — Body — Forms — Death — Theft — Arsenal — Elements — (back to Maw).

## Access cap (owner rule, 2026-10-19)
A run may open only a LIMITED number of trees (v0.1: **5 of 13**). Once the cap is
hit, no new trees open — level-up offers come only from trees already open. Builds
are commitments: pick your neighbourhood on the ring, live with it, win with it.

## Depth under the themes (owner, 2026-10-19)
Each tree is DEEP: a thematic spine (the named powers/forms) wrapped in lots of
stat and skill upgrades — damage %, cooldowns, durations, radii, chances — plus a
shared pool of minor upgrades that can surface in any open tree. Level-up choices
mix majors (build-defining) with minors (build-tuning); the swarm math makes both
matter.

## Tree 13: KART (owner, insisted 2026-10-20)
**MARIO KART is a main tree** — the ring is 13, not 12. The item tree: green
shells (straight shots), red shells (homing), the blue shell (hunts the biggest
threat on screen), banana peels (swarm slips), mushroom boosts, star power
(invincible feast-mode), lightning (shrinks the whole swarm), Bullet Bill
(become the bullet).
