Characters

Characters are who you play in PAC — Judgement Day — each with its own model, its own kit, and a stat home on the pie. There are currently 28 characters with finished model art, and those 28 are this page.

Only created characters are shown. The rest of the cast is filed in the design data — stats, kits, names — but a character that has not been created does not get shown: no model, no listing. Filed characters join this page when their model is drawn. Every image below is the character's actual model, drawn in the locked hand (pencil + coloured pencil on lined notebook paper).
