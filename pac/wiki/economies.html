Scope at a glance CANON

  • Coins are character-based. Tickets are the account-wide one. Rings can be spent on any character.
  • Some of what you gather carries outside the map: a persistent wallet that buys shop goods — or respecs (pay to remove talents you locked in). Runs feed the legend, not just the run.
  • Fruit heals (and can be stored in the food slot, eaten on demand); blue orbs are energy (draft gloss in FULL-SCOPE §21: rings = a carried buffer, coins = shop currency — coins' shop role is confirmed canon above; the ring-buffer gloss is owner-to-confirm).
