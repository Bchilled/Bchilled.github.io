📊 The 7 Stats, Stones & Runes

CANON Wheel order (assistant build per the owner's MTG-wheel model — the owner shuffles as he rules): Strength — Vitality — Spirit — Intellect — Agility — Speciality — Nature — and back around. Placement is strategic: neighbours are good working pairs, never peak synergy; the far side holds opposing strengths and builds. Heroes are placed under their strongest sub-stat — the shade of the sub-stat they lean on hardest. Stat, Slot, Char is the repeating pattern: every stat carries its sub-stats, exactly one + slot (1 per stat — seven stats, seven slots, no stat holds two), its stone, its rune, and its characters.

Threshold law (canon): it takes a certain amount of a stat to get an increase in that area — increases are earned at stat amounts, not granted one-for-one. The amounts themselves are TBD.

Stones

CANON The six Infinity Stones plus the Darkhold as the seventh, one aligned to each stat (table above). Stones are items, not a slot: they sit in inventory and socket only into mythic skills and mythic weapons, and into movement effects — commons and rares hold no stones. Cap: one alteration per slice/stat. Rare resources — extremely rare drops — can be used to craft stones. Eating a stone in-world is also the Level 6 ladder prize (see Mechanics).

Runes and stones are different systems — the two socketables are not interchangeable. Runes = stat grants + hidden combo activations (below). Stones = their own job: slice-colour buffs, socketed into mythic skills, mythic weapons, and movement effects (above). No item holds more than 4 sockets, so a 5–7 rune activation can never live in one item — it spans multiple worn pieces (a worn set). See also Mechanics — gear slots & crafting.

Runes

CANON There are 7 runes, one per stat, in wheel order. Runes are super rare items with a super rare % chance to find. Base function: on their own, runes give stats — you can give stats of any kind to any item, via slots you craft into items. Runes can also work on building items. Combos: combine the right runes in the right item and they activate hidden godly effects — crafted slots + runes are how hidden godly gear is made. The recipes are hidden and are never listed in-game.
