What the coverage exposes

  • Crew banner — 2 possible rolls. The thinnest real item in the game: two base lines, no prefix, and its one listed suffix is contested (conflict #2). If any item needs more rolls, it is this one.
  • Gear ring — 7 possible rolls, all one line deep. A ring can only ever say "+N of one stat". Its 5 contested suffixes (conflict #1) would take it to 12 if the owner rules §5.2 over §6.3.
  • Legs and Boots draw from 2-line base pools — the thinnest pools among armor. Every legs/boots item is those same two lines plus material and affixes; the variety lives entirely in the affixes.
  • Named weapons show 12 — but 3 of those lines (their type profile) are not yet filed per weapon (conflict #5); counted without them so the gap stays visible.
  • Shields sit mid-pack per item (13) but are the thinnest SLOT on the map: 23 filed shields cover only 3 of the 21 stat × shade cells (FILL-MATH) — 18 DRAFT-target cells hold no shield at all.
  • Gloves are the richest slot (22–23) — 5 base lines and 7 eligible suffixes. If balance wants a donor for thin slots, this is the fat end.

Conflicts & open calls this view depends on

  1. Rings vs the suffix pool. §5.2 lists 5 suffixes as ring-eligible (of the Collector, of Fortune, of Greed by name; of Regeneration, of the Well via "jewelry"); §6.3 and algorithm step 7 give rings NO suffix/prefix. Engine follows §6.3. OWNER CALL
  2. Banner vs 'of the Phoenix'. §5.2 lists it banner-eligible; §6.3 says the banner rolls no prefix/suffix (PROPOSED). OWNER CALL
  3. Amulet socket count. Canon: amulets can be slotted; the count is unset. OWNER CALL
  4. Weapon Arm worn piece — which pool? The armor arm piece (bracers, pauldrons…) is counted on the melee weapon-arm table (5 lines); the magic table has 4. OWNER CALL
  5. Named weapons' type profiles are not filed per weapon — and the counts disagree: the JSON shelf list names 35 + 4 gated = 39, while SYSTEMS-ITEMS §3.8 says 42 (38 named + 4 gated). The list on the items page is the 39 actually named. OWNER CALL
  6. Socket caps missing from the canon table for arm pieces, ranged weapons, shields-as-weapons, shields and Exotics — PROPOSED 2s are shown on the items page, flagged in place. Sockets are not roll lines and never enter the counts.

Consumables & eatables (19) are excluded from the ranking on purpose: each does one fixed canon job and rolls nothing — that is the unique-use law, not a gap.
