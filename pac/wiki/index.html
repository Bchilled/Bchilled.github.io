🍕 PAC — JUDGEMENT DAY: The Wiki

What PAC is (canon): a Vampire Survivors-like arena survivor with Pac-Man DNA. You are Pac on big looping maze maps, eating rivers of pellets while a ghost swarm floods the screen — the swarm is the clock: if your clear rate falls behind, you lose. Every enemy is a potential hero unlock, every boss kill opens the next door, and the whole game — stats, worlds, enemies, offers — is filed on one pie chart. “Our pie is our game. It's our Pac.” The intro is canon too: 15 comic-window beats on notebook paper, ending on the title PAC — JUDGEMENT DAY.

The 7-stat pie, in one paragraph

PAC runs on seven stats arranged on a wheel (placement follows the MTG colour-wheel model — neighbours are working pairs, the far side holds your counters): Strength (physical damage) — Vitality (life) — Spirit (recovery, revives, luck) — Intellect (spells, resources, engineering) — Agility (hit chance, dodge, driving) — Speciality (ability power, rage, speed) — Nature (time, weather, poison, shapeshifting) — and back around. Each stat splits into 3 colour shades holding its sub-stats; heroes are placed under their strongest sub-stat. Each stat carries exactly one + slot, one aligned Infinity Stone (the Darkhold is the seventh), and one aligned rune. Seven stats also means 7 universes × 3 worlds = 21 worlds.
