🍕 Worlds — 7 universes × 3 worlds = 21 worlds

CANON 7 pies means 7 universes — one per stat — each with 3 worlds: 21 worlds under the 7-stat system. Each character fits in the worlds, and the villains and monsters are in the game, in the worlds. Above the worlds sits the galaxy map (Universe → World/Planet → Levels, Mario 3-style overworld with nodes and branching paths the player chooses; boss gates are absolute; travel between worlds is by black hole, mouth-first). Themed worlds stock their own mobs per universe.

Boss slots CANON

  • The Emperor — World 1's boss. World 1's boss is THE EMPEROR, whom Pac eats. He is disposable by design: he dies, no design investment. That eating is why Bomberguy was sent.
  • The Ape — Level 6. The first mini-boss waits at the Level 6 gate of the ladder (Bank 4 · Weapon 5 · Stone 6) — see Bosses.
  • Bomberguy — the encore pattern. After every level boss dies, Bomberguy appears for an encore fight and always gets away (bomb-smoke escape, an original never-repeated curse logged in the Curse Ledger) — until the final level, where he doesn't run.
  • Boss gates are absolute everywhere: a win = killing the level boss, and that kill also unlocks the level's Endless option and its unlock pool.

Music CANON spec — per-world tracks TBD

Canon from FULL-SCOPE §16 (there is no separate MUSIC.md yet — this section is the music spec as logged): crazy music, per level — every level/world carries its own track, inspired by iconic video-game and movie music; the universe you're in sets the sound. Treatment: techno versions of everything — whatever the source, it arrives as a techno track; the whole soundtrack lives on the dancefloor, four-on-the-floor under the swarm. Intros and bosses get their own stingers (the Kill Bill-register whistle heralds the assassin in the intro). Music is a level's identity, not wallpaper. Per-world track assignments: TBD.

World sources named in canon as level donors (FULL-SCOPE §§31–34): all Mario worlds/courses, all Sonic zones, arcade classics, Nintendo classics (Contra named), World of Warcraft and Diablo as donor universes, a secret Cow Level (Diablo 2 rules — clearing it unlocks Wendy), and a My Little Pony level. Which donor maps to which of the 21 slots is TBD.
