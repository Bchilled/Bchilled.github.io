The level system

XP and the tempo law

CANON leveling is slower than gathering — gathering pellets outruns levelling; the swarm grows while you gather, so the run's tension is the overrun loop.

DRAFT numbers Balance draft: L2 at 22, then x1.45/level. As built in v3.1.0 (playtest-failed, frozen): 108 x 1.42^(lv-1); time-to-level-2 ~38-50s (harness). The final curve is an OWNER CALL — see the flagged list.

The timer — the swarm IS the clock

the swarm IS the clock; this is the clock you watch — it counts UP from 0:00 (VS-style) and is always visible on the HUD. Level limits run against it:
