🎡 Mechanics

The spins CANON

  • Level 1: the first ability chart spins and lands somewhere — you pick from that 1.
  • Level 2: the second spin lands 1 away from that, on one side or the other — ±1, 50/50 which side.
  • Level 3: the spin lands opposite those — 3 choices opposite.

Because the spins walk neighbours and then jump opposite, each stat is placed strategically on the pie, separated by opposing strengths and builds (MTG colour wheel as the placement model).

The run arc & the Aggressor Law CANON

A run goes from surviving — running, baiting, fighting small battles, being defensive — to feeling aggressive. The flip is the arc. The Aggressor Law (from the owner's playtest): the player must feel like the predator in the maze, not the prey — any tuning state where a run degrades into fleeing is a tuning failure. Pressure rises, and the player's hunting tools (eat windows, scatters, offence) rise with it. The swarm starts few and much slower; speed and numbers climb together until the screen is a storm.

Levels: time limits & Endless CANON

Levels have time limits, walls, and areas to explore. Once a level's boss is defeated, that level gains an Endless option — endless is unlocked per level, by that level's boss kill. A win = killing the level boss; levels clear in order, no skipping. Bosses spawn when the game math judges your build strong enough — and refusing a boss backfires: he becomes a spawner and his escalation timer runs amplified.

Unlock flow CANON

  1. Explore their zone — you don't get access to unlock a hero unless you explore their zone…
  2. …defeat them and the bosses…
  3. …and then get a chance to spin to unlock the hero from that realm. Access is earned; the unlock itself still rolls — it's still always random.

Each boss kill also grants 1 unlock drawn from that level's enemy roster (duplicates → shards). A spin can land on a variant of a hero you already own — variants of owned heroes are possible outcomes, not wasted spins. The pool is lifetime: everything you've ever unlocked stays unlocked.

Distance pricing: buying a hero in a far realm costs more — +5 per distance. (Ring costs also vary by rarity and distance — see Economies.)

Team Up CANON

After 10 levels you unlock Team Up and can tag in a partner. Team size: max 3. The team grows as it scales — scaling is parameterized, growing toward the max of 3.

Gear slots CANON

Each character has: Headpiece · Chest · Gloves · Weapon Arm 1 · Weapon Arm 2 · Legs · Boots · Neck · 2 Rings · Banner (= guild). Dual-wield is standard — two weapon arms is the default loadout, not a perk. Separate from the gear slots, the + loadout is: orb, food, weapon, car, pet, shield, movement effect — one + slot per stat (see Stats). A shield blocks — weapon block % against physical, or spell block against spells — and blocking runs on the shield's own resource, which drains as it works. Fit-first: a shield carried as a weapon (the Captain's) files in the weapon slot; the shield slot is for shields that guard.

Runes and stones are different systems: runes = stat grants + hidden combo activations; stones = their own job — slice-colour buffs, socketed into mythic skills, mythic weapons, and movement effects. The two socketables are not interchangeable.

Consequence: max 4 sockets per item, so 5–7 rune activations span multiple worn pieces (worn sets), never one item.

Crafting CANON

  • Materials — metal, stone, wood and cloth can be found, and are used to craft weapons. Weapons have random stat variables based on type, a small % chance for rare abilities, and 3 qualities (Common, Rare, Mythic) like skills.
  • Rare resources — extremely rare drops — can be used to craft stones.
  • Crafted slots + runes → hidden godly gear: you can craft items to have slots, and combining the right runes into the right item activates hidden godly effects (see Stats & Runes).
  • Weapon access: most weapons can be equipped by most characters; some unlock with X points in that category. Weapon abilities unlock with expertise — use builds mastery, mastery opens the weapon's abilities.

The Darkhold, respec & talent trees CANON

  • Darkhold scrolls: the Darkhold has rare spell scrolls — one-time uses. Some Darkhold spells are enchantments to skills or weapons. Some enchantments even hurt yourself — by design: they feed the big builds that want to take constant damage (rage feeds off being hit).
  • Respec: you can pay to remove talents you locked in — the persistent wallet buys shop goods or respecs.
  • Talent trees: each character has a talent tree with 3 paths. Each character also gets a unique model, unique UI theme, unique skills, and shared commons (commons are shared; rares and mythics are fewer and bigger; a shared ability renamed/reflavoured still carries its signal name — the true function's name).
  • Build Your Own Character is bought with tickets — you pay for every move and everything; looks from a random generator or sliders. DRAFT (assistant) BYOC-PRICING-DRAFT.md proposes price ranges only — no BYOC number is canon until the owner sets it.
