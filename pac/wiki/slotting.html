🧩 Slotting — what holds what, and what goes in it

CANON The loadout has two different systems that are easy to confuse: the 11 worn gear pieces (below) carry sockets, and the + SLOT loadout — exactly 7 slots, ONE PER STAT (owner's law) — holds the character's Food, Weapon, Car, Movement effect, Shield, Pet and Orb. Stones are ITEMS, not a slot (canon).
