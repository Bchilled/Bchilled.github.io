✨ Skills — the 42 elemental skills, as DECISION pages

DRAFT (assistant) Decision state: every skill on this page is IN REVIEW. Nothing here is approved yet. Per the owner's approval loop: a skill is shown, the owner says no → redo, adjusts → "that looks great" → only THEN is it implemented, and it has to be THAT. Until a ruling is recorded on a skill's block, the skill does not exist as far as the build is concerned.

The set (canon shape): 7 elements × Common / Rare / Mythic × (base + variant) = 42 skills. Variants are re-dressed deliveries of the base skill and carry the base's signal name (canon SIGNAL NAME LAW). Numbers sit inside BALANCE.md envelopes and are DRAFT until ruled. Elements: Frost · Fire · Lightning · Psychic · Holy · Nature · Undead — the seven prefix elements (canon).

Two calls block sign-off and are flagged again in the list at the bottom: Holy's stat home (proposed Spirit — Holy/light has no house in the owner's six colour magics) and Death's Ledger's execute band (enemies under 15% life die; bosses immune — DRAFT).
