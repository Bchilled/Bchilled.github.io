The blue orb, for Pac

  • Look: an inverted eclipse — dark core, radiant corona, radiating energy, definitely BLUE.
  • On eating one: his character INVERTS and the screen inverts; enemies FLEE; the ghosts SCATTER outward, panic-dispersed, before the hunt begins (§24).
  • His expression of the surge: he GROWS in size (≥ 1 size step) and gets EXTRA CHOMPY — where other characters' orb buff speaks their language (extra quick, extra strong, quicker cooldowns), Pac's speaks appetite: size and chomp. This is also the Stage-1 teaching beat (eat the big blue one → EAT MODE → first skill).
  • EAT MODE: frightened ghosts are edible; the chain pays 200 / 400 / 800 / 1600 per ghost in sequence (game canon).
