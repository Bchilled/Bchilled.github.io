Pure Tupac (Pure Pac, “the Original”) is one of the playable characters in PAC — Judgement Day. He is the starter character, granted at profile creation, and the slice leader of Maw, filed under Speciality. His starting kit is Hella Maw, Arcade Engine, and the mythic ◆ Transform.

His filed package buys no defense, no HP and no damage: his health is a flat 100 at every level and his chomp gets weaker as he levels. He lives by speed (438 px/s at level 20), by what his kit does at +93% Ability Power, and by a cast-iron stomach that turns the game’s junk food into buffs.

What the math says

His filed package buys zero defense, zero HP, zero damage. EHP is a flat 100 at L1 and L20 alike; standing in the swarm he dies faster every level (5.68s → 0.95s). His survival is not a number on the sheet — it is his speed (438 px/s at L20 against the ghost curve’s 280 px/s ceiling) and the fact that he is never standing still. Pac lives by moving and eating, or not at all.

His basic chomp does not scale. 15.8 DPS at L1, 6.7 at L20 — the chomp is a gathering tool (dots, XP, junk denial), not a weapon. His offense, if it exists, must be carried by his kit, by EAT MODE, and by what he eats — none of which is priced yet.

His real scaling lines are Ability Power (+93% at L20) and Movement Buff potency (+129% at L20). Whatever his abilities and movement effects do, they do it at roughly double strength by L20. Until the kit’s numbers are filed, his L20 is a speed stat with a mouth.

Kit

Three abilities, owner-dictated (ROSTER): one Common, one Rare, one ◆ Mythic. Costs and cooldowns follow the SKILLS-ELEMENTS convention DRAFT — Common 10 · Rare 25 · Mythic burst 50 — paid from his Energy (regen 12/s while not exerting). Rank-ups pay on the Ranking Law axes (cost down · durations longer · range greater · power up · speeds faster); which axes each ability spends its ranks on is not filed for any of the three.

(C) Hella Maw

Effect CANON: eat radius and chomp speed up — “the vacuum mouth the arcade was built on.” Delivery: self-buff / aura class (the maw itself).

Cost / cooldown DRAFT: Energy 10 · cd 2.5s class. Scales with Ability Power (+10.8% → +93.0%) and his attack-speed multiplier (×1.0 — the buff is the only source).

Not filed: eat-radius increase %, chomp-speed increase %, duration, rank-up axis spend.

(R) Arcade Engine

Effect CANON: +1 size step AND a speed surge, together — “bigger AND faster, the two buffs married.” The marriage is the point: the general size law trades size against speed; this ability is the filed exception. Delivery: self-buff, stacking.

Cost / cooldown DRAFT: Energy 25 · Rare band. Not filed: his exact cooldown, speed-surge %, stack cap, per-stack duration, rank-up axis spend.

◆ (M) Transform — Mythic

Effect CANON: shifts form on the mythic dial — “the huge form and its kin” — size-class changing mid-run. It is also his filed equivalent (his car-equivalent: he has no car; the form shift files instead). As a mythic it can socket a stone; his aligned stone is Power → Speciality.

Cost DRAFT: Energy 50 (mythic burst envelope). Not filed: cooldown, rank-up axis spend — and the 13 variables: canon fixes that a mythic carries 13 variables; no file names them. All 13 names and values are missing — the single largest hole in this package.

Elemental skills held: none filed — his kit is character kit, not an elemental set.

Super-unique — Cast-Iron Stomach

CANON Pure Pac resists the bad edibles: their effects are blunted on him, and eating junk grants him buffs instead. He is the one character who can run denial for free — eat a fiending ghost’s drug in front of it and the ghost starves into withdrawal while Pac takes the high himself, tuned to Pac. The blunting magnitude (% reduction) is not filed.

Movement & size

Movement verb: not filed — every character sits under one of the 8 move abilities (Dash · Jump · Fly · Climb · Teleport · Smoke Bomb · Duplicate · Invisible); no file assigns Pac’s. He is filed as on foot, with Transform as his equivalent. His movement expression is filed — raw speed plus Arcade Engine’s surge — the verb itself is not.

Size: eating a blue orb grows him ≥ 1 size step (Orb Law); Arcade Engine adds +1 size step per stack, with speed; Transform shifts size-class outright. Size itself has no exchange rate in the math map. Not filed: the size-step table — how many steps exist, the max, and what each step is worth.

The blue orb

Look: an inverted eclipse — dark core, radiant corona, radiating energy, definitely BLUE.

On eating one: his character inverts and the screen inverts; enemies flee; the ghosts scatter outward, panic-dispersed, before the hunt begins. His expression of the surge: he grows in size (≥ 1 size step) and gets extra chompy — where other characters’ orb buff speaks their language, Pac’s speaks appetite. This is also the Stage-1 teaching beat (eat the big blue one → EAT MODE → first skill).

EAT MODE: frightened ghosts are edible; the chain pays 200 / 400 / 800 / 1600 per ghost in sequence. Not filed: EAT MODE duration.
