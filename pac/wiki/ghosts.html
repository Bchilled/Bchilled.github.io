👻 The 13 Ghosts — colours, drugs, addicts all

CANON All the ghosts from Pac-Man join, each with a unique ability. 13 ghosts, 13 colours, 13 slices, 13 drugs — one ghost per slice of the old 13-slice wheel (the slice names in this table are as filed in DESIGN-ghosts.md; they re-key to the 7-stat positions under the rename rule). The ghosts are drug addicts: every ghost gets off on its drug of choice — the bad edibles are their supply — and the addiction drives its behaviour, not just its paint.

CANON The originals are stronger. Blinky, Pinky, Inky and Clyde are the pure strains — faster, tougher, steadier: their highs are stronger and longer, their withdrawals shallower, their abilities never misfire. The other nine are variations of the originals — higher highs, uglier crashes, abilities that can misfire in withdrawal.

The addiction loop CANON loop; DRAFT mechanisms flagged in source

  1. SOBER — the arcade baseline: canonical targeting (chaser, ambusher, flanker, wildcard temperaments), ability on normal rules, drug timer ticking down.
  2. FIENDING (withdrawal) — targeting degrades, speed stutters, abilities misfire (variations) or dull (originals). Critically: a fiending ghost will break off a chase to score — if its drug is within its sense radius, it beelines for the fix, ignoring Pac, and eats exposed. The longer the fiend, the wider the radius.
  3. HIGH — it scores; the drug's buffs fire, tuned to the ghost. A high ghost is the most dangerous version of that ghost — and the most predictable: the drug is driving, and the drug has one idea.

Supply: bad edibles are pre-salted on maps (dead corners, challenge pockets, near Kirby's shady stock — never inside a clean dot river); Kirby's secret shop sells junk. DRAFT (assistant) mechanisms in DESIGN-ghosts.md §8: eaten ghosts dropping a hit of their drug where they died, and bought junk being droppable as bait. Counterplay (canon system): BAIT — a drop on the map is a leash on one specific ghost. DENIAL — eat the drop first (at full junk price to you) and starve the ghost into full withdrawal. Pure Pac, with Cast-Iron Stomach, is the one character who can deny for free — and takes the ghost's high himself.

EAT MODE, per ghost (summary)

The originals flee like they hunt: Blinky flees at nearly chase speed toward other ghosts; Pinky camps an exit route, invisible while edible; Inky's echoes go blue too (only the real Inky is edible); Clyde's flee path wobbles and drops his Consolation Fruit. Sue doesn't speed up — catch her mid-nod. Funky flees over walls. Kinky splits pre-emptively. Spunky flees while siphoning — priority target, especially if he's carrying your stolen bank item. Full per-ghost EAT MODE notes are in DESIGN-ghosts.md §6; several values there are DRAFT (high Blinky worth double, echoes breaking chain steps, and similar).
