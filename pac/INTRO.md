# PAC — THE INTRO (canonical script, owner's dictation 2026-10-20)

Rule above all rules: **NOT RUSHED.** Timed spacing for dramatic storytelling.
Every beat breathes. This is the first thing that loads.

1. **Darkness.** Stage lighting: a spotlight snaps on and darkens everything
   around it. The text comes in through the light.
2. **The crawl.** "In a galaxy far, far away..." — the log of the rules and the
   story, scrolling up through the spotlight.
3. **The Emperor.** The text tells of the Emperor. Cut to him, laughing, and he
   says: **"DO IT."**
4. **The crime.** Darth Bomberguy uses the Death Star to blow up an
   arcade-booth planet.
5. **Pac's entrance — comedic.** He arrives like a fool and a legend at once,
   and starts eating the crawl text **row by row**.
6. **The narrator fails.** A narrator tries to read the text aloud, can't keep
   up with the eating, and gets cut off mid-word trying to reach the next line.
7. **Growth.** Pac **grows with every row** he eats.
8. **The Death Star.** Fully grown, he eats the entire Death Star.
9. **The exit.** He **burps** — huge — and **farts himself into a black hole.**
10. **The vow.** Darth Bomberguy escapes in the **Q Wing** — a stupid-looking
    ship shaped like a Q — and vows to hunt Pac down.
11. **The landing.** Pac, at his landed planet, sits in a saloon: **The
    Legendary Salty Dawg Saloon**, bright neon lights. He's at the bar drinking
    shots — sad and depressed.
12. **The assassin.** Outside, a **T-1000** comes through a portal, naked,
    sent to kill him.
13. **The snack.** Pac eats him.
14. **The walk.** Pac comes out of the saloon looking like **Arnold in T2** —
    leathers, shades, the walk.
15. **TITLE: PAC — JUDGEMENT DAY.** Then the menu.

Any build of this sequence that drops, reorders, or rushes a beat is wrong.

## Production rule (owner, 2026-10-20)
- STYLE reference = the FIRST animated take: hand-drawn pencil on notebook paper,
  exactly that medium. SCRIPT = this file, beat for beat, directed as written.
- NO improvisation: no invented beats, no substituted gags. If it isn't in this
  file, it isn't in the intro.

## Presentation: comic windows (owner, 2026-10-20)
- The intro — and every cutscene — plays as a COMIC on the notebook paper: framed
  panels with gutters, beats advancing panel to panel, phrases set as narration
  boxes and speech bubbles. The page-turn IS the transition. Same canon beats,
  same unhurried spacing — now they land like panels, not footage.
