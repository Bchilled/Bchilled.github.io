## 1. Weapons, evolutions and paired passives
Sources: https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Weapons · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution · each weapon's own page (e.g. https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Whip), infobox fields.

### Core rules
- Weapons are offered on level-up and in chests. Most are locked behind achievements at first. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Weapons)
- **Inventory limit:** you can't be offered new items once you hold 6 different weapons or 6 different passives. Max-level items aren't offered either. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up)
- **Evolution needs:** (a) the base weapon at max level, (b) its catalyst passive in inventory (any level for classic evolutions; *max level* for Infinite Corridor, Crimson Shroud, Sole Solution, Ashes of Muspell and most post-1.0/DLC evolutions), and (c) opening an evolution-capable Treasure Chest. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution)
- **Which chests can evolve:** in most stages, chests from bosses that spawn at or after **10:00**. Exceptions: the first Mad Forest chest (Glowing Bat, 1:00); every chest in Dairy Plant, Il Molise, Cappella Magna, Boss Rash and Laborratory; most chests in Tiny Bridge. Arcana chests (11:00 and 21:00) only evolve once you're at the max Arcana count. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution)
- **On evolution:** the base weapon is replaced and the passive stays. Usually one evolution per chest. Some chests can evolve up to 3 or 5 weapons (e.g. the Giant Enemy Crab chest; some Il Molise, Bat Country and Tiny Bridge chests). (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution)
- A **Union** merges 2+ maxed weapons into one slot. A **Gift** adds a new item without removing the base weapon. A **Morph** is a character transformation at level 80 plus a relic, and evolves that character's starting weapon with no chest needed. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Evolution)
- **Stat glossary** (from the Weapons page): Area, Speed and Duration are multiplied by the matching player stats. Pierce is enemies hit before a projectile is used up. Hitbox Delay is the time before the same projectile can hit the same enemy again. Knockback is a multiplier. Pool Limit is the maximum projectiles on screen. Chance is the special-effect chance, modified by Luck. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Weapons)

## 3. Level-up offers
Sources: https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Reroll · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Skip · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Banish · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Seal · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Luck · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Limit_Break

- **Choices:** 3, or sometimes 4, unique options (weapons or passives). Picking a new item adds it; picking an owned item levels it up. The game pauses during the choice, and you get a brief invulnerability after picking.
- **4th option chance:** chanceFourth = 1 − 1/totalLuck.
- **Rarity weighting:** each item's weight is its rarity value. P(item) ≈ itemRarity / poolWeight, picked without repeats. With all DLC, the pool weight is 8,130 (weapons) + 1,370 (passives) — **UNVERIFIABLE:** this is the wiki's figure only; no game-data or developer source was found to check it against, and it changes with every DLC/patch. **Base-game-only pool weight: UNKNOWN.**
- **Owned-item bias** (when inventory isn't full): ownedChance = 1 + 0.3x − 1/totalLuck, where x = 2 on even levels and 1 on odd levels. The game checks twice. Each success forces one owned item into the offer.
- **When everything is maxed:** you're offered Gold (Big Coin Bag) or Floor Chicken instead. After receiving 100 Big Coin Bags you unlock "always Chicken" / "always Gold". With **Limit Break** on, you get per-weapon stat upgrades instead, e.g. Whip: Might +0.5% (rarity 10), Area +2.5% (rarity 10, max total 1000%), Amount +1 (rarity 1, max total 20). Evolving a weapon with Limit Break levels resets the extra stats.
- **Reroll:** a new set of choices; the discarded items can't appear in the next set. PowerUp gives +2 per rank, 5 ranks, initial cost 1,000 gold. Ranks unlock by reaching level 80 with Mortaccio, Yatta Cavallo, Bianca Ramba, O'Sole Meeo and Sir Ambrojoe.
- **Skip:** skip the choice and gain experience instead. +2 per rank, 5 ranks, cost 100. Ranks unlock by surviving 15 minutes in Il Molise, 30 minutes in Green Acres, 30 minutes in The Bone Zone, 15 minutes in Moongolow and 15 minutes in Boss Rash. **XP granted per skip: UNKNOWN.** The page's tips text mentions "20% of the experience to the next level", but the sentence is ambiguous and not a stated rule.
- **Banish:** removes an item from level-up choices for the rest of the run. +2 per rank, 5 ranks, cost 100. Ranks unlock by filling 60, 70, 80, 90 and 100 Collection entries. Banished owned items stay at their current level, and chests can't upgrade them.
- **Seal** (meta-banish set in the Collection menu): Seal I/II/III/All, 10 ranks each, initial cost 10,000 each. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/PowerUps)
- **Preserve:** 10% per rank (max 50%) chance that a Reroll, Skip or Banish isn't used up. 5 ranks, cost 500.
- Skips and Rerolls aren't available when your inventory is maxed out. Unlimited stacks are allowed for Reroll, Skip and Banish.
- **Random LevelUp** (unlocked with the Brave Story relic): the game picks for you.
- **Luck** also lowers the chance of map events (swarms, Flower Walls) and raises chest quality. (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Luck)

## 4. XP gems and the XP curve
Sources: https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Experience_Gem · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Level_up · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Growth · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Magnet · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Treasure_Chest

- **Gem tiers by colour:** blue = up to 2 XP, green = up to 9 XP, red = more than that. Exact per-enemy drop values come from each enemy's XP stat (Section 7 has examples).
- **Gem cap:** with more than 400 gems on the ground, no new gems drop. Further XP is merged into a single red gem.
- The XP gained is multiplied by **Growth** (base 100%; additive bonuses).
- The Experience Gem infobox says "Gives 50 XP (as a light source drop)".
- **XP curve (exact wiki rule):**
  - Level 1→2 costs 5 XP.
  - Each level after that costs +10 XP more than the last, up to level 20 (2→3 = 15, 3→4 = 25, ...).
  - Levels 21–40: +13 per level.
  - Level 41 onward: +16 per level.
  - Levels 20 and 40 cost an extra +600 and +2,400 XP. While at those levels you get +100% Growth until you level up.
  - (I haven't computed a full table, to avoid boundary-rounding mistakes. The wiki has a calculator at https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Calculators/Experience.)
- **Magnet** (pickup radius): base 30; modifiers multiply. Formula: Magnet = 30 × character bonus × PowerUp multiplier. The PowerUp gives ×1.25 per rank (2 ranks, so ×1.5625). Attractorb is in Section 2.
- **Vacuum** pickup and the **Gorgeous Moon** evolution collect every gem on the map.
- **Treasure chests:**
  - Contain 1, 3 or 5 upgrades plus gold (1 → 100–200, 3 → 300–600, 5 → 500–1,000 gold at base, multiplied by Greed).
  - Tier odds are set per boss and rolled in order 5 → 3 → 1. chanceWithLuck = levelChance × totalLuck.
  - The first 6 chests in a save are fixed at 1-1-3-1-1-5 items.
  - Example: the Mad Forest 9:00 Silver Bat has 3% (5-item), 10% (3-item) and 50% (1-item) odds.

## 5. Stages and events
Sources: https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Stages · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/The_Reaper · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Mad_Forest · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Enemies

### Structure
- Stage time limits are 30:00, 20:00 or 15:00, depending on the stage.
- **Base-game primary stages: 24** — 5 normal, 7 bonus, 9 challenge, 2 special ("???"), 1 hidden; the table below lists all 24. (Corrected 2026-10-20, F1(b).) The wiki Stages page prose says "20 base game stages", but its own breakdown (5+7+9+2+1) sums to 24, and its totals (34 primary stages = base + 10 DLC-exclusive) also imply 24 (DERIVED: 34 − 10). The "20" is a typo on the wiki page; earlier versions of this dossier repeated it. (81 stages in total including DLC and Adventures, per the same page.)
- Only Mad Forest is unlocked at the start. The rest unlock through achievements; you never buy stages.
- Light sources (destructibles) drop random pickups. Some stages have stage items on the map, plus coffins (character unlocks) and the Merchant.
- **Completion reward:** the run is "cleared" when the timer hits the limit, and you get 500 gold on death after that.
  - Unused Revivals add a bonus. **CONFLICT:** the Stages page says "+100 per unused Revival". The Reaper page says the bonus stacks +100, +200, +300, +400 and then resets to +100 (e.g. 3 revives = 1,100 total).

### The time-limit Reaper rule
- At the time limit (30:00 on normal stages) **The Reaper** spawns, and another one every minute after that, unless Endless is on.
- Reaper stats: **HP 655,350 × player level at spawn**, Power 65,535 (kills on contact), speed 1,200, −50% knockback resistance (it gets pulled *toward* you when hit).
- It resists Freeze (value 1), instant kill and debuffs, but can still be frozen by Clock Lancet, Orologion and similar effects.
- Killing it once unlocks the Mask of the Red Death. Killing it with Infinite Corridor or Crimson Shroud drops 5 Golden Eggs and triggers the White Hand, which ends the run.

### Modes
- **Hyper:**
  - +65% to +75% move speed for player and enemies, +15% to +25% projectile speed, +50% gold. Some stages also add Luck, enemy HP or minimum-spawn bonuses (exact values per stage are in the table below).
  - Unlocks for a normal stage by beating that stage's 25:00 boss. Bonus and challenge stages unlock it automatically.
  - **Minor CONFLICT:** the Mad Forest page lists +100% move speed for Hyper there, outside the 65–75% general range. Treat the per-stage values as authoritative.
- **Hurry:**
  - Doubles the stage clock speed and adds +25% XP (separate from Growth).
  - Timed events happen at the same in-game timestamps, just in half the real time.
  - Unlocked with the Sorceress' Tears relic (Gallo Tower).
- **Inverse:**
  - +200% gold and +20% Luck. Enemies start with +200% HP and gain +5% HP and +0.5 move speed every minute. The map is flipped 180°.
  - The Merchant also sells Skips, Banishes, Rerolls and an extra Arcana.
  - Unlocked with Gracia's Mirror (Eudaimonia Machine).
  - **CONFLICT on speed units:** the Mad Forest infobox lists the Inverse per-minute enemy speed as "+0.005".
- **Endless:**
  - No Reaper. Waves restart, and each loop is one cycle.
  - Each new cycle gives enemies +100% of base HP, +50% spawn frequency and quantity, and +25% damage, and lowers the player's max incoming-damage cap by 1.
  - The Merchant returns each cycle selling a Revival.
  - Unlocked with Seventh Trumpet (Eudaimonia Machine).
- **Other modifiers:**
  - **Arcanas** (tarot modifiers): one at start plus Arcana chests at 11:00 and 21:00. Unlocked with the Randomazzo relic (Gallo Tower).
  - **Limit Break:** Great Gospel relic.
  - **Random Events:** a roulette at :30 each minute picks an event for the next minute. Trisection relic.
  - **Random LevelUp:** Brave Story relic.
- **Stage modifiers:** base-stage and mode bonuses add together, then multiply the matching player stat. Luck is the exception: it's added directly.

## 7. Enemy scaling and behaviour
Sources: https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Enemies · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Curse · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/The_Reaper · https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Stages · individual enemy pages (https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Pipeestrello, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Zombie, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Mudman, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Ghost, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Werewolf, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Mantichana, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Big_Mummy, https://vampire.survivors.wiki/w/Venus)

- **Enemy stats:** Health, Power (contact damage before Armor), Speed, Knockback multiplier, XP. Resistances: Freeze (numeric), Instant-kill (immune or not), Debuff (immune or not).
- **HP × Level** skill (used by bosses and some events): HP is multiplied by the **player's level at the moment the enemy spawns**. It isn't updated afterwards.
- **Curse** (base 100%, additive): raises enemy speed, health, spawn frequency and quantity by its %. effectiveSpawnInterval = spawnInterval / totalCurse.
  - The HP effect applies to new spawns immediately. Other effects start at the next minute.
  - On-screen enemies are hard-capped at 500.
  - Curse sources: Skull O'Maniac (+10%/level, max 50%), Curse PowerUp (+50%), Torrona's Box L9 (+100%), Gold Ring and Metaglio Right (+40% each).
- **Spawn ceiling:** at 300+ live enemies, periodic spawns stop. Only bosses and map events can still spawn.
- **Time scaling:**
  - Normal stages have **no published generic HP-per-minute growth** (UNKNOWN). Difficulty comes from each wave's enemy roster, minimum count and interval (see the Mad Forest table).
  - Scaling rules the wiki does publish:
    - **Inverse:** +200% starting HP, then +5% HP and +0.5 speed per minute.
    - **Endless:** each cycle adds +100% base HP, +50% spawns, +25% damage and −1 to the player's damage cap.
    - **The Bone Zone:** enemy HP +15% and speed +2.5% per minute, uncapped.
    - **Room 1665:** enemy HP scales with player level.
    - Stage-level "Enemy health bonus" and "Enemy minimum" multipliers are shown per stage.
- **Hyper** raises the enemy minimum and speed, and in some stages enemy HP.
- **Map events:**
  - Swarms (fixed-direction rushes across the screen) and Flower Walls (rings that close in).
  - Each repeat rolls chanceWithLuck = eventChance / totalLuck, so Luck makes events rarer.
  - Dairy Plant and Gallo Tower have pressure-plate traps, with cooldown = trapCooldown × totalLuck.
- **Behaviour skills:**
  - *Fixed Direction:* moves in a straight line, no homing.
  - *Floaty* ("Medusa"): wavy movement.
  - Bosses never despawn; they're teleported back on-screen. Bosses can drop chests.
  - Normal enemies spawn just off-screen and can despawn if you move far away.
