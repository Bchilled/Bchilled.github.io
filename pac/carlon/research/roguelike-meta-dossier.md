## 1. Hades (Supergiant Games)
Sources: https://hades.fandom.com/wiki/Charon%27s_Obol · /wiki/Boons · /wiki/Darkness · /wiki/Mirror_of_Night · /wiki/Chthonic_Key · /wiki/Gemstones · /wiki/Nectar · /wiki/Diamond · /wiki/Titan_Blood · /wiki/Wretched_Broker · /wiki/Infernal_Arms · /wiki/Fated_List_of_Minor_Prophecies · /wiki/Achievements · /wiki/Codex · /wiki/Pool_of_Purging · /wiki/Keepsakes

### 1a. What carries over between runs
- **Lost on death:**
  - **Boons** "only last for the duration of a run; they disappear when Zagreus dies".
  - **Charon's Obol** (the in-run shop coin): "Unlike all other currencies, Zagreus will lose all of his Obols when he dies."
- **Kept (all other currencies):**

| Currency | Spent on (meta) |
|---|---|
| Darkness | Mirror of Night talents (35,365 to max everything); Nyx's favor; Resource Director ranks |
| Chthonic Keys | Unlock Mirror talents (65) and weapons (24); 1 key per Mirror reset |
| Gemstones | House Contractor upgrades (32,585 to buy all) |
| Nectar | Keepsakes and character affinity (149) |
| Diamonds | Contractor work orders, decor and music (157 to buy everything) |
| Ambrosia | Max-affinity gifts, companions |
| Titan Blood | Weapon Aspects (306 to max all) |

- **Kept unlocks:** Mirror talents, weapons (Infernal Arms), aspects, keepsakes, companions, Contractor upgrades, Fated List progress, Codex entries.

### 1b. Unlock pacing (first unlocks)
- **Mirror of Night:**
  - 4 talent rows are "Available from start". The first ranks cost 10 Darkness (Shadow/Fiery Presence ranks cost 10/15/20/25/30).
  - Further rows unlock at **5, 10, 20 and 30 Chthonic Keys** (65 total).
  - Green alternate talents unlock after a conversation with Nyx once at least **300 Darkness** has been collected.
  - Refunding (reset) unlocks "after several escape attempts". Exact count: UNKNOWN.
- **Weapons:** the Stygian Blade is default.
  - Heart-Seeking Bow: 1 key. Shield: 3. Spear: 4.
  - Twin Fists: 8 (needs the first four). Adamant Rail: 8 (needs all others).
  - 24 keys total.
- **Aspects** open one run after the Rail is unlocked and at least 1 Titan Blood has been collected.
- **Codex:** obtainable "any time after Zagreus' third escape attempt", but it can take several conversations with Achilles.
- **Pool of Purging** never appears in biomes during the first run of a save, except on post-boss stairways.
- **Titan Blood:** the first clear of the first and final bosses with each weapon pays 12 in total. Heat-level bounties add 240 more, up to Heat 20.

### 1c. Crafting, recycling and salvage returns
- **Wretched Broker (repeatable, one direction only):**
  - Rates: 10 Gemstones → 1 Key · 5 Keys → 1 Nectar · 10 Nectar → 1 Diamond · 2 Diamonds → 1 Ambrosia · 1 Ambrosia → 1 Titan Blood.
  - Gemstone-equivalent prices: Key 10, Nectar 50, Diamond 500, Ambrosia 1,000, Titan Blood 1,000.
  - You can't trade back down or trade Darkness with repeatable trades.
- **Limited Time Offers** (one per run, single use) include better-rate skips, reverse trades and Darkness trades. Examples:
  - 1 Nectar for 10 Gemstones (wiki: 80% saving).
  - 1 Titan Blood for 15 Keys (85% saving).
  - 1 Diamond for 1 Titan Blood (reverse).
  - 50 Darkness for 1 Nectar, 500 Darkness for 1 Ambrosia.
- **Mirror reset:** costs 1 Chthonic Key and refunds **all** spent Darkness. That is a published 100% return.
- **Pool of Purging (in run):** converts up to 3 offered boons into Obols. The amount "is dependent on the rarity and level of the Boon". Exact Obol table: UNKNOWN.
- **Crafting gear:** no gear-crafting system documented beyond Aspect upgrades (Titan Blood) and keepsake levelling. A recycling rate for weapons or keepsakes: UNKNOWN (no such system documented).

### 1d. How achievements and unlock lists are shown or hidden
- **Fated List of Minor Prophecies** (desk scroll, commissioned for 20 Gemstones at the Contractor):
  - 55 tasks that "unlock as the game and storyline progress".
  - Many have a "How to reveal" prerequisite, and **progress is tracked before they are revealed**.
  - Example: the hidden-aspect prophecies appear only after that aspect is revealed.
- **Hidden weapon aspects:** each weapon has one, revealed only when a character speaks a **secret phrase in dialogue**. The player cannot type it; "Zagreus must learn them diegetically". The spear's must come first. Preconditions include investing Titan Blood in that weapon's aspects.
- **Codex:** entries "become more complete" as Zagreus meets, collects, speaks to or slays things. A row of hearts tracks Nectar and Ambrosia gifts per character.
- **Achievements:** 49 (plus a PlayStation platinum). Whether they are flagged hidden on the platform: UNKNOWN.

## 2. Dead Cells (Motion Twin / Evil Empire)
Sources: https://deadcells.wiki.gg/wiki/Cells (Pickups page: Cells, Gold) · /wiki/Collector · /wiki/Blueprints · /wiki/Runes_and_upgrades · /wiki/Blacksmith · /wiki/Boss_Stem_Cells · /wiki/Achievements · /wiki/Blueprint_Extractor

### 2a. What carries over between runs
- **Lost on death:**
  - **Cells** are "lost upon death" unless spent first. You spend them at the Collector (between biomes) or the Blacksmith.
  - **Gold** is lost on death except up to the Gold Reserves amount. The default keeps 0 gold.
  - **Blueprints** are "lost for the run when the player dies before delivering it to the Collector".
- **Kept:**
  - **Collector purchases** "carry over through each run".
  - **Runes are permanent the moment you pick them up** ("there is no requirement to bring it to The Collector").
  - **Legendary Forge** upgrades are "permanent across runs".
  - **Boss Stem Cells** are permanent difficulty toggles. They raise enemy tier and density but also raise cell drops and forge caps, and open bonus doors and new blueprints.

### 2b. Unlock pacing (first unlocks)
- **Collector upgrades and their cell costs:**
  - Health Flask I costs **5 cells** ("Must be unlocked first"). Health Flask II costs 50.
  - Gold Reserves I costs 5, then II 35, III 100 (needs 8 items unlocked), IV 200 (needs 15), V 400 (needs 30, plus a secret blueprint).
  - Recycling I costs 25, Recycling II 120.
  - Random starter weapon, bow and shield cost 65 each and need 2 items unlocked (v3.0 lowered this from 12 items).
  - Backpack costs 100 and needs 15 items unlocked.
- **Blueprint drop rarities:** Always 100% (104 blueprints) · Common 10% (33) · Uncommon 1.7% (53) · Rare 0.4% (64) · Legendary 0.03% (2).
- **Drop cap per biome:** at most 1 item blueprint and 1 outfit blueprint per biome per run. Only the Hunter's Grenade exceeds this.
- **Boss blueprints** drop in a fixed sequence, one per kill. Example: the Advanced Forge I blueprint drops from Conjunctivius on its sixth defeat.
- **First Boss Stem Cell:** beat the Hand of the King (or the Queen, or Dracula) with 0 BSC active.

### 2c. Crafting, recycling and salvage returns
- **Recycling upgrade:** "Transforms objects found on the ground or in chests into gold."
  - Recycling I returns **7% of the sale price**.
  - Recycling II returns **15% of the sale price**.
- **Legendary Forge (Blacksmith):** permanent quality odds bought with cells.

| Quality | Cost to max | Chance per step | Cap by difficulty |
|---|---|---|---|
| + | 500 cells | +5% per 25 cells | 100% on all |
| ++ | 3,000 cells (needs + maxed) | +5% per 150 cells | 50% on Normal; 100% on Hard and up |
| S | 10,000 cells (needs ++ maxed) | +5% per 500 cells | Not investable on Normal and Hard; 25% / 50% / 100% at the higher difficulties |

- **Advanced Forge I:** "increase the quality of an object at the forge twice instead of once" (200 cells, plus a blueprint).
- **"Recycling Tubes":** despite the name, this upgrade lets you choose between starting equipment sets. It is not salvage. Cost: 150 cells, plus a blueprint from the Hand of the King on 1 BSC.

### 2d. How achievements and unlock lists are shown or hidden
- **Achievements:** 108 (6 only on PC/Switch). They can be viewed at the **Scribe** in Prisoners' Quarters. Mods disable them. Whether they are flagged hidden on the platform: UNKNOWN.
- **Secret content found by exploring:**
  - **42 blueprints** don't drop from enemies. They are found in secret areas, puzzles, timed doors, rune-locked areas, locked doors or Daily Run milestones.
  - "Shrewd sleuth" is an achievement for finding your first secret zone.
- **Runes** sit in fixed rooms guarded by an elite or boss. Each one unlocks routes or abilities (e.g. Vine, Teleportation, Ram, Spider, Homunculus, Explorer's). Collected runes show on the save-slot screen.
- **Reveal tools:**
  - **Hunter's Mirror** (150 cells) shows a random enemy or boss carrying a blueprint you don't have yet.
  - **Explorer's Rune** reveals the map and points of interest (not blueprints) after about 80% of a biome is explored.

## 3. Risk of Rain 2 (Hopoo Games / Gearbox)
Sources: https://riskofrain2.wiki.gg/wiki/Lunar_Coins · /wiki/Challenges · /wiki/Scrapper · /wiki/Printer · /wiki/Cauldron · /wiki/Recycler · /wiki/Artifacts · /wiki/Artifact_Hints · /wiki/Logbook · /wiki/Bazaar_Between_Time

### 3a. What carries over between runs
- **Lunar Coins:** "Unlike items or the other form of currency, Gold, Lunar Coins will carry over between games." So items and gold reset every run.
- **Challenges** "permanently unlock new elements": survivors, alternate skills, skins, items and equipment. Unlocked items "start to drop in subsequent runs". Each challenge also awards **1 to 10 Lunar Coins**.
- **Artifacts** (run modifiers) have their own challenge category (20 entries), so they fall under the same permanent-unlock rule.

### 3b. Unlock pacing (first unlocks)
- **Easiest published challenges:**
  - "Advancement" (complete a Teleporter event) unlocks Armor-Piercing Rounds, 1 coin.
  - "Learning Process" (die 5 times) unlocks Tougher Times, 1 coin.
  - "Elite Slayer" (defeat an elite) unlocks Medkit, 1 coin.
  - "The Basics" (discover 10 unique white items) unlocks Crowbar, 1 coin.
  - "Verified" (complete the first Teleporter event 5 times) unlocks MUL-T, 3 coins.
- **Challenge counts on the wiki page:**

| Category | Count |
|---|---|
| Survivors | 14 |
| Items | 30 |
| Equipment | 14 |
| Skills | 65 |
| Skins | 62 |
| Artifacts | 20 |
| **Total** | **205** (DERIVED; counted Oct 20, 2026, includes DLC) |

- **Lunar Coin drop rate:** any kill has a 0.5% chance to drop one. Each drop **halves that chance for the rest of the run**.
- Fixed coin sources: Obelisk obliteration gives 5. With Beads of Fealty, a Twisted Scavenger gives 10 more.
- Survivor examples: "Engineering Perfection" (complete 30 stages) unlocks Engineer. "Warrior" (reach and complete the 3rd Teleporter event without dying) unlocks Bandit. "Washed Away" (beat the game) unlocks Captain.

### 3c. Crafting, recycling and salvage returns (all in-run)
- **Scrapper:** 1 item becomes 1 Item Scrap of the same rarity (White, Green, Red, Yellow), up to 10 stacks per use. Lunar, Equipment, Void and Meal items can't be scrapped. Scrap does nothing when held, but **printers and cauldrons consume it first**. So 1:1, same tier.
- **3D Printer:** takes 1 random item of the displayed item's rarity (scrap first) and gives 1 copy of the displayed item. Usable without limit while you have valid items.
- **Cauldron (tier exchange):**
  - 3 Common → 1 Uncommon.
  - 5 Uncommon → 1 Legendary.
  - 1 Legendary → 3 Common.
- **Recycler (equipment):** turns a dropped item or equipment into a random different one of the same tier. Each item can be recycled **once**. Printed items count as new items, so they can be recycled again. Unlocked by the "Cleanup Duty" challenge.
- **Lunar Coins** buy lunar items in the Bazaar Between Time and unlock Artificer, among other uses.
- Meta-level gear crafting or recycling between runs: none documented (UNKNOWN).

### 3d. How achievements and unlock lists are shown or hidden
- **Challenges** are the game's achievements ("also known as achievements"). Whether the in-game list hides names or conditions before completion: UNKNOWN on the pages I fetched.
- **Exploration-gated challenges:**
  - "Death Do Us Part": discover the hidden chamber in the Abandoned Aqueduct.
  - "Her Concepts": find the Altar to N'kuhana.
  - "Newtist": activate 8 unique Newt Altars.
  - "Pause.": free a survivor suspended in time.
- **Artifacts are fully hidden behind exploration:**
  - Each one needs an **Artifact Code** (shapes found in the world). You enter it at the Compound Generator on Sky Meadow (or Helminth Hatchery), which opens that artifact's trial.
  - The wiki keeps a separate spoiler-free 3-part hint page per code.
- **Hidden Realms** (e.g. Bazaar Between Time, A Moment Fractured) hold shops, cauldrons and coin sources.

## 4. Side note: duplicate-unlock handling (as published)
Clearly labelled side note, per the default. Facts only.
- **Hades:**
  - Titan Blood and Diamond **bounties pay only the first time** a boss is beaten with each weapon at each Heat level.
  - When Heat is too low for bounties, bosses drop a **fixed Darkness amount instead** (Furies 50, Hydra 100, Theseus and Asterius 150, final boss 250). This is a published "repeat clear converts to soft currency" rule.
  - The first Nectar gift to a character gives the Keepsake. Later gifts raise affinity until its cap.
- **Dead Cells:**
  - The Hunter's Mirror points only to blueprints "you don't have yet". Boss drops follow a fixed sequence (one new blueprint per kill).
  - What happens when an already-unlocked blueprint would drop: UNKNOWN.
- **Risk of Rain 2:**
  - Challenges are one-time.
  - The Recycler refuses an already-recycled item. Printing makes a fresh copy that counts as new.
  - A Lunar Coin conversion for duplicates: UNKNOWN (none documented).

## 6. Relevance to PAC canon (mapping only, no design)
Canon (Kanye, 2026-10-20): no account levels. The meta is unlocking levels and characters, crafting and recycling gear, and achievements that are found, not listed.

| Canon question | Where the facts above apply |
|---|---|
| What should survive a PAC death vs reset? | Each game splits currencies into kept and lost: Hades loses Obols and boons (1a); Dead Cells loses unspent cells, gold above the reserve, and undelivered blueprints (2a); RoR2 loses items and gold, keeps Lunar Coins (3a). |
| How does unlocking work without account levels? | All three gate unlocks by currencies, challenges or bosses, not by an account level. Hades uses keys and Darkness (1b); Dead Cells uses cells at the Collector plus blueprints (2b); RoR2 uses challenge completions (3b). |
| How fast should the first unlocks land? | Published first costs and conditions: Hades 10 Darkness and a 1-key bow; Dead Cells 5-cell flask; RoR2 "complete a Teleporter event" and "die 5 times" (1b, 2b, 3b). |
| What return should PAC's gear recycling give? | Published rates: Dead Cells 7% / 15% of sale price to gold (2c); RoR2 1:1 scrap and 3:1 / 5:1 / 1:3 cauldrons (3c); Hades one-way broker ladder and 100% Mirror refund for 1 key (1c). |
| How does gear crafting tie into meta? | Dead Cells Legendary Forge is permanent quality odds bought with cells, capped by difficulty (2c). Hades Aspects use Titan Blood (1a). |
| How are achievements "found, not listed"? | Hades prophecies revealed by prerequisites with progress tracked in advance, and secret-phrase aspects (1d); Dead Cells secret-area blueprints, runes and reveal tools (2d); RoR2 Artifact codes and exploration challenges (3d). |
| What happens when a player earns an unlock they already have? | Side note in section 4: Hades pays bounties once, then gives Darkness. Other games UNKNOWN or one-time. |
