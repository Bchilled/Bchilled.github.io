# Tone — Color, audio, psychology

Brent lock-in (2026-09-07): feel the challenge in palette and ears, not only in numbers.

## Dual poles
| State | Feel | Color | Audio |
|-------|------|-------|-------|
| Safe / thriving | Splendor, relief | Warm greens, soft golds, clear blues | Happy, upbeat — enjoy the island |
| Danger / crisis | Aggressive tension | Reddish filters, crush, pulse | Aggressive; **audio-first**, even subtle before you notice |

## Escalation
1. Pre-threat — quiet dissonance / rising noise floor before bars go critical
2. Rising — pulse creep, warmer to redder grade
3. Peak (storm, bleed-out, heat, starve) — aggressive mix, red pulse (slow, not strobe)
4. Recovery — audio resolves first, then color returns to splendor

## Crisis accents
Storm, bleed, heat, cold, starve share danger language but get distinct accents.

## Rules
- Audio leads danger; color confirms; bars explain
- Safe music must feel good to sit in
- Avoid constant max intensity
- Accessibility: slow pulse + non-color cues
