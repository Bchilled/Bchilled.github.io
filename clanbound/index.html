Walk north up the Ember Trail into Emberhold. Elder Bramm waits in the Clan Hall at the north end of town — choose your first beast at the standing stones: Fernhorn, Embercub, or Surgekit. To leave town, follow the dirt path: the south gate leads back down the Ember Trail, and the east gate opens onto the Highgrass Trail — taller grass, stronger beasts — ending at Stonehaven, where Elder Vesk and a mender wait. That is the end of this demo's road.
Weaken a wild beast first, then throw an Oathstone (the BIND command) to bring it into the clan. Mender Sola's hut, southwest in Emberhold, heals your party for free.
