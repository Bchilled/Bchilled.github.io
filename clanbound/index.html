Walk north up the route to Emberwick. The clan elder waits in the hall at the top of town — choose your first beast at the standing stones, then head back to the tall grass to battle and bind wild beasts. Weaken a beast first, then throw a Binding Stone (the BIND command) to bring it into the clan. The mender's hut, southwest in town, heals your party for free.
