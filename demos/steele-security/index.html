Services

Book one service for an evening, or keep a team on retainer for a client, family or venue.

Close protection

A protection officer with your client from door to door, in plain clothes or a suit. Advance checks of every venue and route before the day.

Car service

Black sedans and SUVs with trained drivers for airport runs, meetings, dinners and nights out. The driver waits for the client, not the other way round.

Concierge service

Reservations, tickets, travel bookings, deliveries and errands handled by one contact who knows your client's preferences.

Club and venue service

Door staff, guest-list management and VIP hosts for private clubs, lounges and members' events. Smart dress, calm manner.

Client care

A named team lead for each client. One call arranges the driver, the protection and the table, and we confirm every detail in writing beforehand.
